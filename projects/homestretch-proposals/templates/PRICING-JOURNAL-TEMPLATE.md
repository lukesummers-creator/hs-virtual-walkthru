1) Choose production: painter-days → **labor cost anchor L**
2) Choose fee mode + margin target:
   - Standard: f = franchise + card fee
   - RealVitalize: f = franchise + RV fee (NOT card fee)
3) Materials target is priced off customer price: **materials = m × P**
4) Solve customer price:
   - Allowed cost pool = **P × (1−f) × (1−g)**
   - Allowed cost pool must cover labor + materials: **L + (m×P)**
   - Therefore: **P = L / ( (1−f)(1−g) − m )**
