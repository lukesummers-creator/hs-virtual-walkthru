## Proposed internal *draft* unit-times (bias-for-action)
Use these when FastField is vague and we need a number for a draft proposal pack.

### Baseboards (install/replace)
- Starting point: **$6 / LF labor** (install/replace, paint-grade) + **$1 / corner**
- Minimums:
  - One-room minimum: **$250 labor** (mobilization + setup)

### Door casing
- Starting point: **$150 labor per opening** (both sides) OR **$75 labor per side**

### Water-damage jamb/casing repair
- Starting point: **$250–$500 per opening** depending on severity (repair vs full replace)

## When to force a clarification (high-leverage questions)
- Linear feet of baseboard per room OR room perimeter
- # of corners (inside/outside)
- Remove existing? (yes/no)
- Paint included? (yes/no)
- Material (MDF vs pine; profile height)
- Openings: one-side casing vs both sides
- Water damage: repair bottom section vs full replace
