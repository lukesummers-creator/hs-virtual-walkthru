Bedroom 1: Remove shelving to allow proper rehab work. Remove wallpaper; plaster repair (unknown behind wallpaper); dark-to-light prep; prep/paint walls and ceiling; prep/paint existing trim (baseboards/casings). Paint built-in (approx 2 ft × 8 ft; includes (2) cabinet doors).

Shelving reinstall: Shelving will not be reinstalled unless explicitly added as an add-on scope item.

Crown strip recommendation: Recommend removing the ~1" crown strip for the cleanest finish. Removal may reveal additional repair needs.

Pegboard recommendation: Recommend removing the pegboard paneling for the cleanest finish and best wall repair access.

Optional Add-On — Paint Pegboard Paneling: Prep/prime/paint pegboard paneling in place. Coverage cannot be guaranteed due to perforations/texture; additional coats may be required for acceptable coverage.

Plaster unknown note: Condition behind wallpaper is unknown; if removal reveals additional plaster failure requiring expanded skim/mesh/patch beyond typical, it may require a change order. We will notify and get approval before any added scope.

Patch and paint includes filling nail holes and minor drywall repair. Two coats of paint are used on painted surfaces unless otherwise specified. Paint and materials are included in Service Quote.
