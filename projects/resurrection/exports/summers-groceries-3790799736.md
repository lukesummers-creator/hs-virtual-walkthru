- **2026-02-02T14:13:01** — **Mac** (id:28, type:message)
  Best way (juicy + still tastes like rotisserie): reheat it gently, covered, with a little moisture. Two solid options:
  
  Option A (best overall): Oven, covered
  
  1. Preheat 325°F.
  2. Put chicken pieces (or whole chicken) in a baking dish.
  3. Add 2–4 Tbsp liquid to the dish (chicken broth, a splash of water, or even a little melted butter).
  4. Cover tight with foil.
  5. Heat until warm:
  • Pieces: ~15–25 min
  • Whole chicken: ~25–35 min
  6. Optional: uncover for the last 3–5 min to re-crisp skin (watch it).
  Tip: If you have a thermometer, pull it when the thickest part hits about 150–165°F (it’s already cooked—don’t bake it to death).
  
  Option B (fast + very juicy): Microwave with “steam” cover
  
  1. Cut into pieces (reheats more evenly).
  2. Put on a plate, cover with a damp paper towel or microwave lid.
  3. Microwave at 50–70% power in 30–45 sec bursts until warm.
  4. If you want crisp skin, hit it in a hot pan 1–2 min after.
  Avoid (dries it out)
  
  • High heat (400°F+) for long
  • Reheating uncovered
  • “Just nuke it on high” for several minutes
  If you tell me whether it’s whole vs pieces, I’ll give you the exact timing/game plan for your setup.
